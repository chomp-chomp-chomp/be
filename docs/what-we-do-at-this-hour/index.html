The world is asleep, and so I move quietly through it, one cookie at a time.

While the cookies bake, I clean. Bowls, counters, trays. The work is simple, and yet necessary. A clean kitchen is a clean mind. And a dozen cookies, arranged in imperfect rows on the cooling rack, is a small triumph over impermanence. Even a monk would nod.
