Step Challenge = Smør & Dominique Ansel

May 5, 2026

I recently visited Smør in Williamsburg, Brooklyn — a Scandinavian bakery with cardamom buns and all kinds of amazing things. This is their second location; the original, in the East Village, was started in 2019 by two Danes. I've lived in my neighborhood for a long time, and I had no idea this location existed until I stumbled upon it on a walk around my neighborhood during a step challenge at work. I remember when the bakery used to be occupied by an HVAC company with a large garage door — I always wondered why they needed it. Air conditioners aren't that tall.

Smør has made good use of the 3,000-square-foot space, and that old garage door opening can be opened up during nice weather. It functions as both an R&D lab and a counter-service bakery, and the kitchen is open-concept — you can see the workers making the pastries. Their mixers are enormous (see image below). They've got fresh sourdough, rye bread, pastries, sandwiches, and rotating loaves including dark chocolate-cherry, olive, seeded, and heritage grain varieties like Einkorn and Danko rye, plus Danish pastries like Hindbærsnitter (raspberry shortbread) and Drømmekage ("Dream Cake"). I got a cardamom bun, a cinnamon bun, and a fantastic Scandinavian donut and felt like I'd really accomplished something.

I hope you've heard of the Cronut, created at Dominique Ansel Bakery in SoHo. As it happened, still doing a work step challenge, I walked past it, thought "that's cool, never been there," and kept going. Then I realized: What am I doing? I'm never in SoHo. So, I turned around and walked on in. While it's most famous as the birthplace of the Cronut — Ansel's half-donut, half-croissant creation filled with ganaches and jams, with a new flavor each month — it's also the birthplace of the DKA (Dominique's Kouign Amann), the Cookie Shot, Frozen S'mores, and Blossoming Hot Chocolate. I picked up the cronut of the month — strawberries and cream brown butter — plus a pistachio crème-filled, pistachio-encrusted piece of heaven. The cronut lived up to the hype.

I'd attempted to visit Radio Bakery in Greenpoint this past Sunday, but I was thwarted by the Five Boro Bike Tour — Greenpoint was cut in half and I couldn't get past the blockades. It'll happen.
